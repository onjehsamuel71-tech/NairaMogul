# Background Music

Drop your background music file here as `theme.mp3`.

The `AudioProvider` loads `/audio/theme.mp3` on first user interaction
(browsers block autoplay until the user clicks or taps).

## Requirements

- **Format:** MP3 (broadest browser support). OGG also works if you change
  the `src` in `startBackgroundMusic`.
- **Length:** 60–120 seconds, looped seamlessly.
- **Volume:** Normalize to around -14 LUFS; the player sets volume to 0.25.
- **Licensing:** Use royalty-free or licensed music. Do NOT ship copyrighted
  Afrobeats tracks without a license.

## Free sources

- https://pixabay.com/music/
- https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/
- https://freemusicarchive.org/

Rename your chosen file to `theme.mp3` and place it in this folder.
